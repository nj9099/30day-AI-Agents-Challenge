# Chicken Broccoli Rice Bowl

## Description

A simple high-protein chicken and broccoli rice bowl.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked rice
- 1 lb chicken breast
- 2 cups broccoli
- 1/2 cup onions
- 1 tablespoon cooking oil
- 1 teaspoon garlic
- 1 teaspoon soy sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

## Dietary Information

- High protein
- Meat based
- Dairy free
- Contains soy

## Preparation Time

10 minutes

## Cooking Time

20 minutes

## Servings

4

## Instructions

1. Cut the chicken breast into bite-sized pieces.
2. Heat the cooking oil in a large skillet.
3. Cook the chicken until fully cooked.
4. Add the onions and broccoli.
5. Cook until the broccoli becomes tender.
6. Add garlic, soy sauce, and black pepper.
7. Serve the chicken and broccoli over cooked rice.
