# Broccoli Stir Fry

## Tags
vegetarian, quick, dairy-free, low-calorie

## Ingredients
- 2 heads broccoli, cut into florets
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp chili flakes (optional)
- salt and pepper to taste

## Servings
2

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a wok over high heat.
2. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
3. Add broccoli and onion, stir-fry for 5-6 minutes.
4. Add soy sauce and chili flakes, toss to coat.
5. Season with salt and pepper and serve.

## Notes
- Serve over rice or noodles for a full meal.
- Add tofu or chicken for extra protein.
