# Chicken Fried Rice

## Description

A high-protein fried rice made with chicken, eggs, rice, broccoli, and onions.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked rice
- 1 lb chicken breast
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup broccoli
- 1/2 cup onions
- 1 tablespoon cooking oil
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

## Dietary Information

- High protein
- Meat based
- Contains eggs
- Contains soy
- Dairy free

## Preparation Time

10 minutes

## Cooking Time

20 minutes

## Servings

4

## Instructions

1. Cut the chicken breast into small pieces.
2. Heat the cooking oil in a large pan.
3. Cook the chicken until fully cooked.
4. Add the onions and broccoli and cook until slightly tender.
5. Push the ingredients to one side of the pan.
6. Add the eggs and scramble them.
7. Add the cooked rice.
8. Add soy sauce, garlic, and black pepper.
9. Mix everything together and cook for several minutes.
10. Serve hot.
