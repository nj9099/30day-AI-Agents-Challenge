# Chicken Rice Bowl

## Tags
high-protein, dairy-free, meal-prep, quick

## Ingredients
- 2 chicken breasts
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 1 head broccoli
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- salt and pepper to taste

## Servings
2

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
20 minutes

## Instructions
1. Dice chicken and season with garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Cook chicken until golden and cooked through, about 7 minutes.
4. Remove chicken and sauté onion and broccoli until tender, about 5 minutes.
5. Serve chicken and vegetables over rice.

## Notes
- Stores well in the fridge for up to 3 days.
- Swap broccoli for any green vegetable you have.
