# Creamy Chicken Pasta

## Description

A creamy chicken pasta dish made with chicken, pasta, cheese, and cream.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken breast
- 12 oz pasta
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1/2 cup onions
- 1 tablespoon cooking oil
- 1 teaspoon garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

## Dietary Information

- High protein
- Meat based
- Contains dairy
- Contains gluten

## Preparation Time

10 minutes

## Cooking Time

25 minutes

## Servings

4

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
2. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
3. Heat the oil in a large skillet.
4. Cook the chicken until fully cooked.
5. Add onions and garlic.
6. Add heavy cream and Parmesan cheese.
7. Stir until the sauce becomes creamy.
8. Add the cooked pasta.
9. Mix everything together.
10. Serve hot.
