# Egg Fried Rice

## Tags
quick, budget-friendly, dairy-free, high-protein

## Ingredients
- 3 cups cooked rice (day-old works best)
- 3 eggs
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 cup frozen peas (optional)
- salt and pepper to taste

## Servings
2

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Instructions
1. Heat sesame oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat.
2. Scramble the eggs and set aside.
3. Sauté onion until softened, about 3 minutes.
4. Add rice and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until heated through.
5. Add soy sauce and toss to coat.
6. Fold in scrambled eggs and peas.
7. Season with salt and pepper.

## Notes
- Day-old refrigerated rice fries better than fresh.
- Add any leftover protein for extra substance.
