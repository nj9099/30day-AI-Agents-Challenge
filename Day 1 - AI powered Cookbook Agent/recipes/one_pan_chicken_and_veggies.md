# One Pan Chicken and Veggies

## Tags
high-protein, meal-prep, dairy-free, low-effort

## Ingredients
- 4 chicken thighs
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 onion, quartered
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- salt and pepper to taste

## Servings
4

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
30 minutes

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Toss broccoli and onion with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan.
3. Season chicken thighs with paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
4. Nestle chicken among the vegetables.
5. Drizzle remaining olive oil over chicken.
6. Roast for 25-30 minutes until chicken is cooked through.

## Notes
- Use any vegetables you have on hand.
- Great for batch cooking 4 days of lunches.
