# Simple Omelette

## Tags
high-protein, quick, breakfast, low-carb

## Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 1/4 onion, diced
- 1/2 cup broccoli, chopped small
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- salt and pepper to taste

## Servings
1

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
5 minutes

## Instructions
1. Beat eggs with salt and pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
3. Sauté onion and broccoli for 2 minutes.
4. Pour in eggs and cook until edges set, about 2 minutes.
5. Fold omelette in half and cook 1 more minute.

## Notes
- Add any cheese if dairy is not restricted.
- Works great as a quick meal-prep breakfast.
